Szechuan Pork & Green Bean Stir-Fry

Dinner · Easy · 22 min (10 prep + 12 cook) · Makes 1 serving (~70 g protein). Use the planner's servings stepper to batch-cook.
High-proteinLow-carbGluten-freeDairy-free

Szechuan Pork & Green Bean Stir-Fry delivers 70 g of protein for just 10 g net carbs — a quick dinner that comes together fast. A filling, macro-balanced dinner that won't spike your blood sugar.

70 gProtein
10 gNet carbs
26 gFat
4 gFiber
554Calories

Ingredients

  • 290 g Lean pork strips
  • 150 g Green beans
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce, garlic & chili
  • 2 tsp Sesame oil
  • Spring onion & Szechuan pepper, to taste

Equipment

  • non-stick skillet

Method

  1. Blister the green beans in a hot wok, then set aside.
  2. Stir-fry the pork until browned, add garlic and chili.
  3. Return the beans, splash in soy and toss; finish with spring onion.

Storage & make-ahead

Fridge: keeps 3–4 days in an airtight container — reheat gently. Most portions freeze well for up to 2 months; thaw overnight.

Tip

Punchy and fast. Dial the chili up or down to taste.

Dietary swaps

Soy-free: Soy sauce, garlic & chili → coconut aminos (note: a couple of these shift the protein source slightly)
