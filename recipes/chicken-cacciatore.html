Chicken Cacciatore

Dinner · Medium · 35 min (10 prep + 25 cook) · Makes 1 serving (~70 g protein). Use the planner's servings stepper to batch-cook.
High-proteinLow-carbGluten-free

Chicken Cacciatore delivers 70 g of protein for just 12 g net carbs — ready in about 35 minutes. A filling, macro-balanced dinner that won't spike your blood sugar.

70 gProtein
12 gNet carbs
22 gFat
4 gFiber
526Calories

Ingredients

  • 230 g Chicken breast
  • 150 g Chopped tomatoes
  • 150 g Mushrooms, pepper & onion
  • 20 g Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tsp Olive oil, garlic & herbs

Equipment

  • non-stick skillet
  • saucepan

Method

  1. Brown the chicken in olive oil, then set aside.
  2. Soften the mushrooms, pepper and onion; add garlic, herbs and tomatoes.
  3. Return the chicken and simmer until cooked and the sauce thickens; finish with parmesan.

Storage & make-ahead

Fridge: keeps 3–4 days in an airtight container — reheat gently. Most portions freeze well for up to 2 months; thaw overnight.

Tip

A rustic Italian braise that reheats brilliantly for lunches.

Dietary swaps

Dairy-free: Parmesan cheese → nutritional yeast or vegan parmesan (for lactose intolerance or a dairy-free diet)
