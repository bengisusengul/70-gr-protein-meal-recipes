Protein Hot Chocolate

Snack · Easy · 8 min (5 prep + 3 cook) · Makes 1 serving (~70 g protein). Use the planner's servings stepper to batch-cook.
High-proteinLow-carbGluten-freeVegetarian

Protein Hot Chocolate delivers 70 g of protein for just 12 g net carbs — ready in about 8 minutes. That's enough high-quality protein in one sitting to clear the leucine threshold for muscle repair.

70 gProtein
12 gNet carbs
8 gFat
3 gFiber
400Calories

Ingredients

  • 60 g Whey or casein protein powder (chocolate)
  • 400 ml Skim milk
  • 16 g Powdered peanut butter (PB2)
  • Cocoa powder & sweetener, to taste

Equipment

  • saucepan

Method

  1. Warm the milk gently (don't boil).
  2. Whisk in the protein, PB powder, cocoa and sweetener until smooth and frothy.
  3. Pour into a mug and enjoy warm.

Storage & make-ahead

Best blended fresh. Pre-portion the dry ingredients ahead so it's a 60-second job when you want it.

Tip

Casein protein makes an especially creamy, slow-digesting bedtime hot chocolate. 70 g protein in a mug.

Dietary swaps

Dairy-free: Whey or casein protein powder (chocolate) → pea or other plant protein powder; Skim milk → soy, oat, or almond milk (for lactose intolerance or a dairy-free diet)

Nut-free: Powdered peanut butter (PB2) → sunflower-seed butter (e.g. SunButter) (for nut allergies — seeds work just as well)
