Mustard-Glazed Pork Tenderloin with Green Beans

Dinner · Easy · 32 min (10 prep + 22 cook) · Makes 1 serving (~70 g protein). Use the planner's servings stepper to batch-cook.
High-proteinLow-carbGluten-freeDairy-free

Mustard-Glazed Pork Tenderloin with Green Beans delivers 70 g of protein for just 7 g net carbs — ready in about 32 minutes. It keeps net carbs low and added sugar near zero, so your energy stays steady.

70 gProtein
7 gNet carbs
18 gFat
4 gFiber
470Calories

Ingredients

  • 270 g Pork tenderloin
  • 150 g Green beans
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • Garlic, rosemary, salt, pepper, to taste

Equipment

  • non-stick skillet
  • oven + sheet pan

Method

  1. Heat oven to 200C/400F. Rub the tenderloin with mustard, garlic, rosemary and salt.
  2. Sear in an oven-safe pan to brown all sides, then roast 12-15 min to 63C/145F; rest.
  3. Toss green beans in olive oil and roast or steam until tender. Slice the pork and serve.

Storage & make-ahead

Fridge: keeps 3–4 days in an airtight container — reheat gently. Most portions freeze well for up to 2 months; thaw overnight.

Tip

Pork tenderloin is one of the leanest cuts available, making this the lowest-calorie dinner here at a full 70 g protein.
