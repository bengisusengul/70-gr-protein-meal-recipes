Cheesy Turkey Burgers with Slaw

Dinner · Easy · 24 min (12 prep + 12 cook) · Makes 1 serving (~70 g protein). Use the planner's servings stepper to batch-cook.
High-proteinLow-carbGluten-free

Cheesy Turkey Burgers with Slaw delivers 70 g of protein for just 8 g net carbs — minimal fuss, maximum protein. A filling, macro-balanced dinner that won't spike your blood sugar.

70 gProtein
8 gNet carbs
28 gFat
4 gFiber
564Calories

Ingredients

  • 250 g Lean ground turkey (93%)
  • 1 Large egg
  • 30 g Cheddar cheese
  • 120 g Cabbage & carrot (slaw)
  • 1 tbsp Light mayo & mustard

Equipment

  • non-stick skillet
  • grill pan

Method

  1. Mix the turkey with the egg and seasoning; form patties and grill or pan-cook.
  2. Melt the cheddar over the patties in the last minute.
  3. Toss the shredded cabbage and carrot with mayo-mustard and serve alongside.

Storage & make-ahead

Fridge: keeps 3–4 days in an airtight container — reheat gently. Most portions freeze well for up to 2 months; thaw overnight.

Tip

Serve bunless over the slaw, or in a lettuce 'bun'. The egg keeps lean turkey juicy.

Dietary swaps

Dairy-free: Cheddar cheese → dairy-free cheese (for lactose intolerance or a dairy-free diet)
