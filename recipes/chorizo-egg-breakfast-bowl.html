Spanish Chorizo & Egg Breakfast Bowl

Breakfast · Easy · 20 min (8 prep + 12 cook) · Makes 1 serving (~70 g protein). Use the planner's servings stepper to batch-cook.
High-proteinLow-carbGluten-free

Spanish Chorizo & Egg Breakfast Bowl delivers 70 g of protein for just 5 g net carbs — minimal fuss, maximum protein. Exactly the kind of meal that makes a high-protein, low-carb week easy to stick to.

70 gProtein
5 gNet carbs
35 gFat
2 gFiber
615Calories

Ingredients

  • 4 Large eggs
  • 150 g Liquid egg whites
  • 90 g Lean cooking chorizo
  • 30 g Manchego or cheddar
  • 80 g Red pepper & onion
  • Smoked paprika & parsley, to taste

Equipment

  • non-stick skillet

Method

  1. Crisp the sliced chorizo in a dry pan, then add diced pepper and onion and soften.
  2. Pour in the whisked eggs and egg whites; stir gently to soft scramble.
  3. Fold through the cheese, scatter with parsley and serve in a bowl.

Storage & make-ahead

Best assembled fresh. Prep the components up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled, and combine just before eating.

Tip

Chorizo brings smoky flavour and fat, so this one is richer; pair with a light lunch.

Dietary swaps

Dairy-free: Manchego or cheddar → dairy-free cheese (for lactose intolerance or a dairy-free diet)
