Korean Beef Bulgogi Cauliflower Bowl

Lunch · Easy · 22 min (12 prep + 10 cook) · Makes 1 serving (~70 g protein). Use the planner's servings stepper to batch-cook.
High-proteinGluten-freeDairy-free

Korean Beef Bulgogi Cauliflower Bowl delivers 70 g of protein for just 13 g net carbs — minimal fuss, maximum protein. It keeps net carbs low and added sugar near zero, so your energy stays steady.

70 gProtein
13 gNet carbs
26 gFat
4 gFiber
566Calories

Ingredients

  • 270 g Lean beef strips
  • 150 g Cauliflower rice
  • 60 g Kimchi
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce, garlic, ginger & sesame
  • 20 g Spring onion

Equipment

  • non-stick skillet

Method

  1. Marinate the beef in soy, garlic, ginger and a little sweetener, then sear hot and fast.
  2. Warm the cauliflower rice and top with the beef.
  3. Add kimchi, spring onion and a sprinkle of sesame.

Storage & make-ahead

Best assembled fresh. Prep the components up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled, and combine just before eating.

Tip

Big Korean BBQ flavour without the sugar or rice. Sub sweetener for the usual sugar in the marinade.

Dietary swaps

Soy-free: Soy sauce, garlic, ginger & sesame → coconut aminos (note: a couple of these shift the protein source slightly)
