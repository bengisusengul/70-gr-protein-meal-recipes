## 📝 Full description (rich-text body)

**Stop improvising three meals a day. Let a chef hand you the plan.**

You don't fail at eating well because you lack willpower. You fail because you're **hungry**,
**bored**, and **winging it** three times a day. This Reset fixes all three:

- High-protein meals kill the hunger — protein is the most filling food there is.
- A rotating, genuinely tasty menu kills the boredom.
- And a fully-planned 28 days kills the improvising. The thinking is already done.

Four weeks isn't magic — but it's long enough to *feel* the difference (steadier energy, fewer
cravings, clothes fitting better) and to turn "I'm trying to eat better" into "this is just how I
eat now." That second thing is the whole prize.

### What you get

**1. The 4-Week Reset Program Guide (PDF)** — your 28-day roadmap, written like a chef talking you
through it: a focus, a habit and a chef's tip for every week; day-by-day meal plans (~210 g protein
a day); the science distilled to 90 seconds; setup, eating-out and troubleshooting; and progress
tracking that isn't just the bathroom scale.

**2. The Printables Pack (PDF)** — print it, stick it on the fridge, tick as you go: 4 weekly
meal-plan grids, 4 ready-made shopping lists grouped by aisle, a 28-day habit tracker, and a weekly
check-in & measurements log.

**3. The complete 100-Recipe Cookbook (PDF) — included free** — the full library every plan is built
from, yours to keep: all 100 high-protein, low-sugar recipes with a photo, macros and dietary swaps
for each, 27 vegetarian options, and the complete cited science.

**Plus** lifetime access to the free planning app at the70gprotein.com — scale any recipe to your
bodyweight and auto-build your shopping list in one tap.

### How the 4 weeks work

- **Week 1 — Foundation:** Get the system in. Just hit protein at every meal, without overthinking it.
- **Week 2 — Momentum:** Beat the boredom that kills most diets, with brighter Mediterranean flavours.
- **Week 3 — Lean & Light:** Dial it in — maximum protein, lowest calories, the cleanest week of the four.
- **Week 4 — Lock-In:** A favourites tour — then learn to build every week yourself, for good.

### By the end of 28 days

✓ No more 3pm energy crash or sugar cravings
✓ Genuinely full on real food — no willpower battles
✓ Clothes, especially waistbands, fitting more easily
✓ Cooking high-protein on autopilot — you stop needing the plan
✓ A repeatable system you can run any time you drift

### Who it's for

Anyone who wants to eat more protein and less sugar without living on sad chicken and broccoli — busy
people, beginners, vegetarians (plenty of meat-free 70 g meals), and anyone who's bounced off
restrictive diets before. No gym or special equipment required; everyday supermarket ingredients only.
