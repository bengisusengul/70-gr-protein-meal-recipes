# 💬 Warm-network messages — your first 10–20 sales live here

_Written 2026-06-10. Send these over the next 3–4 days. Personal messages convert 10–50× better
than any ad. Rule of thumb: the more personal the channel, the less it should look like marketing._

**The order that works:**
1. Day 1 — closest friends & family (Template A)
2. Day 2 — foodie friends, ex-colleagues, anyone who's ever asked you for a recipe (Template B)
3. Day 3 — Instagram story sequence (Template D) + Facebook post (Template E)
4. Day 4 — the broader email/LinkedIn circle (Template F)
5. Always — within a day of any sale: the review ask (Template G)

**Two rules:**
- Send individually, never broadcast lists (people can tell).
- Ask for ONE thing per message: either *try the free plan*, *buy*, or *share* — not all three.
