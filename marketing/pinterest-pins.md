# Pinterest pins — titles, descriptions & destinations

Keyword-rich copy for the 12 pins in `pins/`. Pinterest is a **search engine** (most food searches
are unbranded), so titles and descriptions are written for search, with the protein number as the hook.

## Setup
- Use a **Business account** (free) so you get Rich Pins + analytics. Convert at pinterest.com/business/convert.
- Enable **Rich Pins** once (validate any recipe URL at the Rich Pins validator) — the recipe pages
  already have Open Graph + Recipe JSON-LD, so they qualify.
- Suggested **boards** (create these, keyword-named): *High-Protein Dinners* · *High-Protein Breakfast*
  · *High-Protein Lunch & Meal Prep* · *Low-Carb High-Protein Recipes*.
- **Destination:** each pin links to its recipe page (free value + relevance; the page funnels to the
  free 7-day plan and the products). To grow the email list faster, you can repoint 2–3 pins to
  `https://the70gprotein.com/free-plan.html` instead.
- Post a few per day rather than all 12 at once; re-pin to multiple relevant boards over time.

Base URL: `https://the70gprotein.com/recipes/`
