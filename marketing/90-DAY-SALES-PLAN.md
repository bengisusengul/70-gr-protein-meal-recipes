That is not a criticism — it's the diagnosis. **The plan's #1 job is consistency, not cleverness.**

**Strategy in one line:** chef-on-camera videos + Pinterest + 100 free recipes drive attention →
the free 7-day plan captures emails → email nurtures to the £12 cookbook and £29 Reset → reviews
compound trust → repeat.

## 2. THE DO LIST — where the hours go (ranked)

### 1️⃣ Short-form video — TikTok + Reels + Shorts (≈8–10 h/wk) — THE engine
- **What:** 5–7 videos/week, cross-posted to all three. Month 1 content = the existing
  `30-day-content-calendar.md` (already written, day by day, with hooks and CTAs).
- **Why #1:** food is the most-watched short-form category; a real chef cooking real 70 g-protein
  plates is the strongest card we hold. It's also the only channel that can go non-linear (one
  breakout video can beat 3 months of everything else).
- **How to win:** batch-film 3–4 on Mondays · hook in the first 1.5 seconds (the calendar's hooks
  do this) · end every video with "full recipe free on my site — link in bio" · reply to every
  comment for the first hour after posting.
- **What good looks like:** month 1 = consistency + learning which hooks work (expect most videos
  under 500 views — normal). Month 2 = double down on the 2–3 formats with the best retention.
- **Reality check:** posting daily for 2 weeks then stopping = zero results. 5/week for 13 weeks
  = 65+ videos, and the algorithm rewards the back half far more than the front.

### 2️⃣ Pinterest (≈30 min/wk after the queue) — the compounder
2 pins/day until the 22-pin queue is done (~11 days), then 3–5 fresh pins/week (Claude generates
new batches on request — we have 100 recipe photos). Pinterest is a search engine: pins keep
working for months. Business account → watch outbound clicks in analytics.

### 3️⃣ Email (≈1 h/wk) — the converter
One broadcast every Wednesday: 1 recipe + 1 chef tip + 1 soft CTA (`broadcast-templates.md` has
July written; works on Kit's free tier). Every other channel's job is to feed this list — the list
is the only audience you own. When subs pass the gate (§4), upgrade Kit and the 5-email
welcome→sell sequence switches back on automatically.

### 4️⃣ Reddit (≈45 min/wk) — spiky free traffic
The 6 posts in `reddit-posts.md`, 1–2/week, exactly as written: warm the account first (comment
genuinely for a few days), post full value (whole recipe in the post), site only in your profile /
when asked. One good Reddit post can drive 1–3k visits and a wave of email signups.
**Never link-drop — one ban costs more than ten posts earn.**

### 5️⃣ SEO collections — built ✅ (0 h/wk — already done in this session)
8 new collection pages are now live targeting mid-tail searches ("high protein breakfast ideas",
"vegetarian high protein recipes", "high protein meal prep"…), interlinked from all 100 recipe
pages, the hub and the homepage, sitemap resubmit pending. **Honest expectation:** a new domain
takes 3–6 months to rank — this is a month-4-to-9 payoff we plant now. Resubmit `sitemap.xml` in
Search Console this week (2 min).

### 6️⃣ Social proof (≈15 min per sale) — the multiplier
Review-ask (template G) within 24 h of EVERY sale, no exceptions. At 3+ reviews: quote strip goes
onto reset.html (placeholder is already in the code) and the Gumroad listings. Reviews are the
single biggest conversion lever a £12–29 product has.

### 7️⃣ Two REAL sales moments (planned, honest, one each)
- **Mid-August — founding price ends:** the £12 cookbook was always a founding price; it moves to
  £19 on **Sunday 17 August**. Announce it 2 weeks out (email + videos + pins), remind at 3 days
  and 24 h. Real deadline, kept forever — this is the ethical urgency that works.
- **September Reset Challenge:** cohort starts **Monday 8 September** ("back to routine" energy).
  Everyone who buys the £29 Reset by Sep 7 does the 4 weeks *together* — you post along daily,
  buyers get the challenge emails. 2-week push (Aug 25 → Sep 7), then the challenge itself
  generates 4 more weeks of content + testimonials. (Claude builds the challenge emails + video
  scripts in late August.)

### 8️⃣ Months 2–3 only: borrowing audiences
Once you have ~4 weeks of content + a scoreboard to show: 5–10 shoutout-swap DMs with similar-size
food/fitness creators, and 2–3 pitches to small fitness/meal-prep newsletters (offer a free
exclusive recipe). Skip entirely in July — you need proof-of-work first.

## 3. THE DON'T LIST — money and time savers

| ❌ Don't | Why |
|---|---|
| **Paid ads** (Meta/Google/TikTok) | At a £12–29 price with zero conversion data, cold-traffic CAC will exceed the product price. Revisit only after: organic creative that provably converts + email funnel numbers + ideally a bundle ≥£49. Not this quarter. |
| **Buy followers, engagement pods, fake/incentivised reviews** | Kills the account algorithmically and the brand ethically. Gumroad bans fake reviews. |
| **Build new products** (membership, book #2, app features) | You have a complete 3-tier ladder. The bottleneck is attention, not product. Membership gets considered only after the Reset sells weekly (see gate, §4). |
| **Build more marketing assets before executing existing ones** | The June engine sat unused for 3 weeks. New assets are procrastination wearing a productive outfit. Claude will say no to asset requests that duplicate unexecuted ones. |
| **Add more platforms** (X, Facebook, LinkedIn, Threads, blog…) | 3 channels done for 13 weeks beats 7 done for 2. Cross-posting shorts to 3 apps is already the efficient version. |
| **Constant discounts / fake countdown timers** | Trains people to wait; fake urgency measurably destroys trust. We run exactly two real moments (§2.7). |
| **Chase head-term SEO** ("high protein recipes") | A 1-month-old domain cannot win those in 90 days. The collections target winnable mid-tail; recipes target long-tail. Patience. |
| **Redesign the site / fiddle with branding** | The site is good. Every hour there is an hour not filming. Conversion work happens only if the data says traffic converts badly (gate, §4). |
| **Paid PR, press releases, influencer marketplaces** | Near-zero ROI at this size. Borrowed audiences come free via §2.8. |
| **Fly blind / go by feelings** | Feelings say "nothing is working" in week 3 and "we're famous" after one good video. The scoreboard says the truth. 30 min every Sunday. |

**Focus:** the scoreboard picks the winners — double down on the 2–3 best video formats; kill what's
dead. Collab/newsletter outreach starts. **Aug 3:** announce the price rise · **Aug 17:** cookbook

**Outcome targets:** +15–35 sales (price-rise week should be the best sales week yet) ·
list at **200–400** · first collab/shoutout landed.
**⚙️ Kit gate:** upgrade to **Creator annual ($390/yr)** when subs > ~250 **or** the first £100
revenue month — whichever comes first. (Re-activates the automated welcome→sell sequence.)

### 🗓 SEPTEMBER — "The Reset Challenge" (weeks 10–13)
**Focus:** Aug 25–Sep 7 challenge launch push (emails + daily shorts) → cohort runs Sep 8–Oct 5
with you posting along → testimonial harvest → quote strip onto reset.html + Gumroad.
**Outcome targets:** +20–40 sales, Reset-weighted (higher £/sale) · list **400–700** ·
5–10 challenge testimonials · quarter revenue running total:

| Scenario | Sales (qtr) | Revenue (qtr) |
|---|---|---|
| Floor (consistent but unlucky) | ~25 | **~£400** |
| Expected (consistent execution) | 45–75 | **£700–£1,500** |
| Stretch (a video breaks out) | 100+ | **£2,000–£4,000** |

**Say it plainly:** this quarter's real prize is the *system* — 65+ videos, 50+ pins, 400+ emails,
8 ranking pages, reviews. Months 4–6 is where compounding pays; most people quit in month 2.

**Gates / kill-switches (check monthly):**
- Videos average <200 views after 4 *consistent* weeks → change hooks/formats (Claude analyzes),
  don't add channels.
- >500 visits/month but email opt-in <2% → fix free-plan placement/copy BEFORE chasing more traffic.
- Any channel with zero signal after 6 honest weeks → cut it, reinvest the hours in video.
- Membership tier: only when the Reset sells ≥5/week for 3 straight weeks. Not before.

**Setup: ✅ done (Jul 4)** — GoatCounter live at **the70gprotein.goatcounter.com**.
Dashboards to bookmark: GoatCounter (visits + `buy-*` click events) · Gumroad sales · Pinterest
analytics (business) · TikTok/IG insights · Kit subscribers · Search Console (monthly is enough).

**The funnel and what each number means:**
```
views (social) → profile taps → site visits → free-plan opt-ins → subscribers
                                     ↓                                ↓
                            buy-* click events  →  Gumroad sales ← emails
```
- Views but no site visits → CTA/bio problem. Visits but no opt-ins (<2%) → free-plan page problem.
- Opt-ins but no sales → email/offer problem (needs the Kit sequence back on).
- Buy-clicks but no sales → Gumroad page/price problem. Each has a different fix — that's why we measure.
