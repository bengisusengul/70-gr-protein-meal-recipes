- Rule: gold for the number ("70 g"), white for the words around it.

## 4. Voice (the 6 rules)

1. Taste-first: the food is the hero, protein is the punchline.
2. Chef authority, zero hype-bro tone. British English.
3. HIGH-PROTEIN, NOT carnivore/keto. 27 of 100 recipes are vegetarian. No medical claims, ever.
4. Radical honesty: the app is free and we say so in the sales pitch. No fake urgency, no fabricated reviews.
5. Numbers are real: macros come from `js/recipes-data.js`, never rounded up or invented.
6. Every CTA points at ONE thing (usually the free 7-day plan).
