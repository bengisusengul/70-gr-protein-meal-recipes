# 📅 30-Day Content Calendar — short-form video (Reels / TikTok / Shorts)

_Written 2026-06-10. One video per day for 30 days. Every script is grounded in a real recipe
from the app (macros are exact). Goal: traffic → free 7-day plan → email funnel → £29 Reset._

---

## The system (read once)

**Where to post:** film ONCE, post the same vertical video to **Instagram Reels + TikTok +
YouTube Shorts** (3 platforms, zero extra work). All three reward food content.

**The one link:** put `the70gprotein.com/free-plan.html` in your bio on day 1.
Every caption ends with the same CTA — consistency is what makes it convert.

**Filming workflow (chef-efficient):**
- **Batch it:** one cooking session = 3–4 videos. Film Sunday + Wednesday, post daily.
- Phone, vertical 9:16, window light, wipe the counter. That's the whole setup.
- Every video: **big text overlay in the first second with the protein number** ("70g PROTEIN").
  The number is the brand. Auto-captions ON (most people watch muted).
- 15–30 seconds. Shorter = better. No intros, no "hey guys" — first frame is food or the hook line.
- Voiceover optional: you can record it after, while the video is on screen. Your accent +
  "professional chef" framing is the differentiator — say it often.

**Posting checklist (2 min/day):**
1. Post at a consistent time (UK evening ≈ 5–7pm works for food).
2. Caption from this file + hashtags.
3. Reply to EVERY comment in the first hour (the algorithm watches this). Reply to recipe
   questions with: "Full recipe free on my site — link in bio 🔗"
4. If a video outperforms (2× your usual views), make 2 more in that exact format next week.

**Weekly rhythm (so it never feels random):**
- **Week 1 — Meet the chef + easy wins** (trust, free plan pushes)
- **Week 2 — Meal prep & the app** (utility, save-able content)
- **Week 3 — Chef authority & myth-busting** (shares, follows)
- **Week 4 — The Reset week** (soft sell → direct offer, still 70% value)

**Hashtag base set (pick ~6 + 2 recipe-specific):**
`#highprotein #highproteinrecipes #lowcarb #lowsugar #mealprep #proteinrecipes #healthyrecipes #ukfood`
